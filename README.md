### Pitch
**The Observer** is a FPS puzzle game. You play as a test subject trapped in a looping simulation. Your goal is to complete the "observation tests" assigned by the System.

### MDA

#### **Mechanics**
- **Controls:** Move with WASD and Click with the Mouse.
- **Goal:** Find a random object within a time limit.
- **Tools:** Flashlight (Level 3) and Sprint.
- **Challenge:** Some levels are too bright or too dark to see clearly.
- **System:** Item locations are random every time.

#### **Dynamics**
- **Time Pressure:** The timer makes the player move fast.
- **Observation:** Players must look from different angles to find hidden items.
- **Difficulty:** The game changes from relaxing to stressful/scary.
- **Replayability:** Because items move, players cannot memorize the map.

#### **Aesthetics**
- **Visual Style:** Starts Low Poly, then becomes glitchy and spooky.
- **Mystery:** A feeling of "something is wrong" with the simulation.
- **Uncanny Valley:** The final level reuses the first level's assets but with distorted effects.

### Features
- **4 Unique Levels:**
  - *Tutorial:* White box room with 3D text instructions.
  - *The Room:* Standard gameplay.
  - *The Park:* Over-exposed lighting challenge.
  - *The Mine:* Pitch black darkness requiring a flashlight.
- **The Red Room (Finale):** A horror version of the first level with distorted audio and visuals.
- **Narrative Briefing:** "System" messages appear at the start of each level.
- **Double Ending:**
  - *Normal Ending:* Follow orders and exit the door.
  - *Hidden Ending:* Interact with the mirror to break the simulation.
